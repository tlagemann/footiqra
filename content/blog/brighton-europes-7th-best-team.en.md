Brighton & Hove Albion are the seventh-best football team in Europe right now. Not seventh in the Premier League: seventh among all 132 clubs in Europe's top seven leagues. In the Footiqra power ranking they sit ahead of Atlético Madrid, Liverpool, Inter and Borussia Dortmund.

That sounds like a stretch. Here is the evidence, and the one honest catch.

## Proof 1: they beat the best

Brighton have played Europe's number one and number five this season and beaten both:

- **Brighton 3-0 Arsenal** (19 September). Our model gave Brighton a 31% chance of winning. Arsenal lead the Premier League and the power ranking.
- **Manchester City 0-1 Brighton** (31 October). Brighton's win chance before kick-off: 23%.

Beating the top two of your own league is the strongest signal a rating system can get. Upsets against strong opponents move ratings more than anything else.

## Proof 2: they win big

Brighton have won 4-0 against Aston Villa, 5-0 at Coventry and 5-0 against Crystal Palace. With a goal difference of **+17 after nine games**, they lead the Premier League on that measure, ahead of Manchester City (+10) and Arsenal (+9).

Goal difference matters because big wins say more about strength than narrow ones. The Footiqra rating counts a three-goal win clearly more than a one-goal win.

## Proof 3: it is not a nine-game fluke

Brighton did not come from nowhere. Before the first matchday, carried over from last season, they were already **tenth in Europe**. Nine strong games have lifted them three places, not twenty.

They sit third in the Premier League with 19 points, two behind Manchester City and five behind Arsenal. Our simulations give them a 10% chance of winning the title.

## The catch: the Premier League bonus

There is one fair objection. Every club's rating includes the strength of its league, and the Premier League is Europe's strongest by a distance (see [which is Europe's strongest league](/blog/strongest-league-in-europe/)). Its clubs get about 56 points more league strength than La Liga clubs.

That bonus is what puts Brighton ahead of Atlético. On club form alone, Atlético would be in front. Against Liverpool, there is no bonus to explain anything: both play in the same league, and Brighton are ahead purely on results, even though Liverpool beat them 2-0 last month.

So the fair version of the headline: **Brighton are the third-best team in the best league in Europe, and that makes them one of the seven best teams on the continent.**

## Will it last?

Two things to watch:

- **No European football.** Brighton only play league matches, so they can focus on the Premier League. It also means their rating rests on fewer games than that of Champions League clubs.
- **The next weeks look kind.** Brentford, Hull City and Newcastle are next up. Strong results there would keep Brighton in the top ten.

## FAQ

### Are Brighton really one of the best teams in Europe?

By results this season, yes. Brighton rank seventh in the Footiqra power ranking, which compares all clubs from Europe's top seven leagues on one scale.

### Why are Brighton ranked above Liverpool?

Brighton have more points (19 to 15) and a much better goal difference (+17 to +2), including wins against Arsenal and Manchester City. One head-to-head defeat does not outweigh that.

### Can Brighton win the Premier League?

Our simulations give them a 10% chance as of 3 November 2026, behind Arsenal and Manchester City.
