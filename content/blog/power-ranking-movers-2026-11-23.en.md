One result defined the week in the Footiqra power ranking. Leeds United went to Stamford Bridge as outsiders and won **4-1**. Leeds gained 20.5 rating points, more than any other club in Europe's top seven leagues, and climbed from 19th to 15th. Chelsea lost exactly the same amount and dropped eleven places, from 22nd to 33rd.

## Leeds stun Stamford Bridge

Before kick-off on 21 November, our model gave Chelsea a 47% chance of winning at home, Leeds 29%. A win for the underdog would have moved the ratings anyway. A three-goal margin away from home moved them much further, because the model weighs [the size of a win](/blog/football-ratings-glossary/) as well as the result.

For Chelsea, the defeat was costly in two ways: they lost exactly the points Leeds gained, and they fell out of the top 30. See the full [match page](/matches/chelsea-fc-vs-leeds-united-fc-2026-27/) for the forecast and the head-to-head record.

## Brighton's bad Sunday

Brighton were 48% favourites at Hull City on 22 November and lost 2-0. That cost them 17.9 points, the third biggest loss of the week. Hull climbed seven places to 35th, Brighton slipped from 7th to 8th. They are still in the top 10, but the gap to the clubs behind them has narrowed.

## FAQ

### Why did Leeds gain so many points from one win?

Leeds won away from home against a higher rated team, and they won by three goals. Both increase the rating change: an upset moves ratings more than an expected result, and a bigger margin moves them more than a narrow one.

### Why did Chelsea lose exactly as many points as Leeds gained?

The Footiqra rating works like Elo: points move from one team to the other. Whatever one side gains in a match, the other side loses.

### How often is the power ranking updated?

Several times a day. Every finished match in the seven covered leagues and the Champions League changes the ratings of both teams.
