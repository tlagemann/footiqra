---
title: How Much Is Home Advantage Worth in Football?
description: We analysed 4,725 matches from Europe's top seven leagues. Home advantage is worth about a third of a point per game, and it varies a lot by league.
published: 2026-11-05
---

Every football fan knows that playing at home helps. But how much, exactly? We went through every league match from the last two complete seasons in the seven leagues Footiqra covers: 4,725 games from 2024/25 and 2025/26.

The short answer: home teams collect about **0.35 more points per game** than away teams. Over a season, that is worth roughly six to seven points. Enough to decide a title race, a European place or a relegation battle.

## Home advantage in numbers

Across all 4,725 matches:

| Outcome  | Share |
| -------- | ----- |
| Home win | 43.2% |
| Draw     | 25.4% |
| Away win | 31.4% |

Home teams averaged **1.55 points per game**, away teams **1.20**. Home sides also scored more: 1.54 goals per game against 1.27 for the visitors.

Put another way: of all league points handed out, **56.4% went to the home team**. If playing at home made no difference, that figure would be 50%.

## Which league has the biggest home advantage?

Home advantage is not the same everywhere. Here are the seven leagues, ranked by the share of points won by the home side (2024/25 and 2025/26 combined):

| League                                     | Home win | Draw  | Away win | Points per game (home / away) | Home share of points |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------- | ----- | -------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------- |
| [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga/)               | 46.7%    | 25.0% | 28.3%    | 1.65 / 1.10                   | **60.0%**            |
| [Eredivisie](/leagues/eredivisie/)         | 44.9%    | 25.7% | 29.4%    | 1.60 / 1.14                   | 58.5%                |
| [Ligue 1](/leagues/ligue-1/)               | 46.6%    | 22.3% | 31.1%    | 1.62 / 1.16                   | 58.3%                |
| [Primeira Liga](/leagues/primeira-liga/)   | 42.2%    | 26.5% | 31.4%    | 1.53 / 1.21                   | 55.9%                |
| [Premier League](/leagues/premier-league/) | 41.7%    | 25.9% | 32.4%    | 1.51 / 1.23                   | 55.1%                |
| [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga/)         | 41.2%    | 24.9% | 33.9%    | 1.49 / 1.27                   | 54.0%                |
| [Serie A](/leagues/serie-a/)               | 39.3%    | 27.2% | 33.4%    | 1.45 / 1.27                   | **53.3%**            |

**La Liga stands out.** Spanish home teams won nearly half of their matches and took 60% of all points. In 2025/26 alone the figure reached 62.2%, the highest single season of any league in our data.

**Serie A has the smallest home advantage.** Italian away teams pick up 1.27 points per game, the joint best away record of the seven leagues alongside the Bundesliga.

The gap between the top and bottom of this table is bigger than it looks. In La Liga, home teams earn 0.55 more points per game than away teams. In Serie A, the difference is only 0.18.

## The clubs that live and die at home

Some clubs depend on home advantage far more than others. In the 2025/26 season:

- **Barcelona won all 19 of their La Liga home games.** Away from home they averaged 1.95 points per game, still excellent, but a full point less.
- **Elche** took 1.84 points per game at home and just 0.42 away, the biggest gap of any club in the seven leagues.
- **Mallorca** (1.74 home, 0.47 away) and **Atlético Madrid** (2.42 home, 1.21 away) show the same pattern. Four of the five most home-dependent clubs play in Spain.

And then there are the clubs that seem to prefer travelling:

- **Bologna** averaged 1.16 points per game at home and 1.79 away.
- **Tottenham** managed only 0.79 points per game at home, but 1.37 on the road.
- **Wolfsburg** took 0.59 points per home game and almost twice as many, 1.12, away.

Single seasons like these are partly noise. A handful of results can flip a club's home and away record. But they show how much a team's points total can depend on where its matches are played.

## What about this season?

The 2026/27 season is still young, and with 641 league matches played by early November, the samples are small. Still, two leagues currently break the pattern:

- In the **Eredivisie**, away teams are ahead: 1.42 points per game against 1.30 for home sides.
- In the **Bundesliga**, home advantage is unusually strong so far: 1.68 points per game at home, 1.10 away.

Both numbers will probably drift back towards the long-term average as the season goes on.

## Why does home advantage exist?

Researchers usually point to a mix of factors: crowd support, familiar surroundings, no travel, and referee decisions that may lean slightly towards the home side under pressure from the crowd. Matches played without fans, as during the pandemic, are often cited as evidence that the crowd is a big part of the effect. Our data cannot separate these causes, but it clearly shows the combined result.

## How Footiqra accounts for home advantage

Every rating model has to deal with home advantage, otherwise it would overrate teams that have just played lots of home games. In the Footiqra rating, the home side gets a bonus of **65 rating points** when the expected result of a match is calculated. Between two equally rated teams, that gives the home side an expected score of about 59%, where a draw counts as half a win.

That bonus flows into everything we publish: the [power ranking](/), the [match predictions](/predictions/) and the title race simulations. Matches on neutral ground get no bonus. The full details are on the [methodology page](/methodology/).
