The Premier League is Europe's strongest league, and its lead has grown. That is the verdict of the Footiqra power ranking as of 15 November 2026. Ligue 1 stays second ahead of La Liga, the Bundesliga has moved back above the average of the seven leagues, and in the race for sixth place the Primeira Liga has overtaken the Eredivisie.

## The league strength ranking

League strength measures how strong a league is compared with the others, based on results in European competition since 2024/25. Zero is the average of the seven leagues. [Why league strength matters](/blog/why-league-strength-matters/) explains the idea.

## Premier League: further ahead than before

The Premier League is first by every measure: league strength, average club rating and clubs at the very top. Nine of the 20 highest-rated clubs in Europe play in England, led by Arsenal at number one. Seventeen of the top 50 are English.

How big is the lead? A gap of 59 points to Ligue 1 means that two otherwise equal clubs would give the Premier League side an expected score of about 58% on neutral ground. Against the Eredivisie, the gap of 171 points means about 73%.

## Ligue 1 ahead of La Liga

Ligue 1's second place is still the biggest surprise compared with the UEFA coefficients, where France is only fifth. Its strength comes from European results since 2024/25, including Paris Saint-Germain's Champions League title in 2025. PSG are second in the power ranking, but they are the only French club in Europe's top 20. Ligue 1 gets its strength from depth: twelve of the top 50 clubs are French.

La Liga had gained more than any other league in early autumn, but it has given back 17 points since 2 November. The gap to Ligue 1 has widened to 20 points. Barcelona and Real Madrid are still among Europe's best, in third and fifth place.

## Bundesliga and Serie A move up

The Bundesliga has gained 10 points in two weeks and is now above the average of the seven leagues. Bayern are fourth in Europe, the best German club. Only 10 points separate the Bundesliga from La Liga.

Serie A is still fifth, but it has gained the most since 2 November, 13 points. By UEFA coefficient it is Europe's second-strongest league, and its gap to the Bundesliga is down to 9 points. Inter are eighth in Europe, Como tenth.

## Primeira Liga ahead of the Eredivisie

Both leagues sit well below the big five, which fits their UEFA coefficients. The Eredivisie has lost 13 points this season and has dropped to last place. Portugal's best clubs are doing better: Porto are 17th in Europe, Sporting 22nd and Benfica 25th. PSV, the best Dutch club, are 29th.

## How reliable is this?

League strength rests on comparatively few matches: 34 games this season have connected two of the seven leagues so far. A few results in the Champions League can still move the order. The closest races right now are La Liga, Bundesliga and Serie A, which are only 19 points apart. We will update this ranking as the season goes on.

## FAQ

### What is the strongest football league in Europe right now?

The Premier League, according to the Footiqra power ranking as of 15 November 2026. It leads on league strength, average club rating and the number of clubs among Europe's top 20.

### Is La Liga stronger than Serie A?

Yes, currently by about 19 points of league strength. La Liga ranks third, Serie A fifth.

### How is league strength calculated?

It starts from UEFA's association coefficients and then moves with every European match between clubs from two covered leagues. Domestic matches never change it.
